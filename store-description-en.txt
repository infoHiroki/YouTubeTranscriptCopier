【Short Description】
Copy YouTube transcripts with AI prompts in one click. Also fetches video title, URL, channel, and description to boost AI output quality. Supports English & Japanese with customizable prompt templates.

---

【Detailed Description】
YouTube Transcript Copier lets you copy YouTube transcripts together with AI prompts to your clipboard in one click — ready to paste into Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, or any AI tool.

Key Features:
· One-click transcript copy with your AI prompt
· Fetch video metadata: title, URL, channel name, and description (each toggleable)
· Save and manage frequently used prompt templates
· Free-input mode for custom one-off prompts
· Two themes: Clean Modern and Cyberpunk
· English and Japanese interface support

Including video metadata alongside the transcript gives AI tools richer context, resulting in more accurate summaries, better structured notes, and higher quality output overall.

Perfect for students, researchers, content creators, and anyone who wants to get the most out of YouTube with AI.
